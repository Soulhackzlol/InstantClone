      Danger zone
      Restore InstantClone to a clean state.
      Reset settings
      Ports, buffer, overlays folder, trace log, password and stream key, hotkeys, MIDI, crash protection and General options go back to defaults. Destinations, profiles and integrations are kept.
      Reset everything
      Also wipes destinations, profiles, integrations and your Discord, phone and Twitch connections. Then this page reloads into the setup wizard. Cannot be undone.
      What's not touched

      The buffer file on disk is kept (the next launch starts a fresh empty one anyway). InstantClone stays registered in OBS. Unregister from the OBS tab if you also want to remove it from OBS's Service dropdown.
