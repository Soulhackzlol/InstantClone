Twitch VOD audio
Keep copyrighted music in your live stream but out of the VOD. OBS locks its built-in VOD Track to the service literally named "Twitch", so on the InstantClone service you add the second audio track with a tiny optional script.
  1. Press Download and pick where to save optional-vod-unlocker.lua (your OBS scripts folder is tidiest).
  2. In OBS: Tools → Scripts → +, pick optional-vod-unlocker.lua (one time).
  3. Set the script's VOD audio track to the OBS mixer track carrying your VOD audio (route it in Advanced Audio Properties). Your live mix stays on track 1.
Then, per destination, use Audio track to route it: Twitch keeps Both, send Track 2 (music stripped) to YouTube to dodge copyright.

Twitch login app
Integrations log in to Twitch through an app registered at Twitch. InstantClone brings its own, so most people never touch this. Use your own if you prefer to, or if Twitch shows as unavailable.
Your app's Client ID
  1. Open dev.twitch.tv/console and log in. Twitch asks you to turn on two-factor authentication first.
  2. Name: anything, for example My InstantClone. It can't contain the word "Twitch".
  3. OAuth Redirect URL: http://localhost It isn't used, but Twitch requires one.
  4. Category: Broadcaster Suite. Client Type: Public. This one matters: a Confidential app can't stay logged in.
  5. Press Create, then Manage, copy the Client ID and paste it above. You don't need a secret.
Changing the app logs your Twitch accounts out. After saving, connect them again in Integrations.
