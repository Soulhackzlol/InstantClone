What these numbers mean

Frames sent: video and audio pieces sent to all destinations this session. Data sent: how much that adds up to.

Delay cuts: times the delay was cut back toward live. Each cut lands on a keyframe, so viewers see no glitch.

OBS drops: times OBS disconnected from InstantClone, for example when you stopped and started streaming.

Reconnects: times a platform connection dropped and InstantClone connected it again. The Destinations tab says why one failed.

Memory: how much memory InstantClone itself uses right now.
