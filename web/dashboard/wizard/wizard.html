Enhanced Broadcasting Twitch feature

Twitch's multi-track quality ladder (1080p / 720p / 480p…) so every viewer gets a smooth stream. It just works: InstantClone passes it on untouched. Nothing to set here.

How to turn it on in OBS

In OBS: if you registered InstantClone as a service (on the OBS step), open Settings → Stream, keep Service on InstantClone and turn on Multi-track via InstantClone, left on Auto.

On Custom RTMP, Enhanced Broadcasting needs OBS older than 32.2: use OBS tab → Legacy after setup. On newer OBS, register the InstantClone service instead.

How to turn it on in Streamlabs

In Streamlabs' Go Live window, turn on Enhanced Broadcasting in the Twitch section. Left off, InstantClone switches it on for that stream with a single video track, so the stream still comes through.

Separate VOD audio Twitch feature

A second audio track for your Twitch VOD: keep copyrighted music in the live stream but out of the recording. Optional, and you can do it later in System → Twitch & OBS.

How to set it up in Streamlabs

Streamlabs has it built in: Settings → Output, turn on Twitch VOD Track (under Streaming in Advanced mode) and pick the audio track without the music. It comes through InstantClone as it is.
