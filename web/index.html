How it works

InstantClone appears next to Twitch / YouTube in OBS's Settings → Stream → Service list. Multi-track Video "Auto" works out of the box — pick InstantClone, restart OBS, and stream.

Edits OBS's services.json in %APPDATA% with a .bak backup. You can unregister any time from the OBS tab.

🔧 Custom RTMP server

Paste these in OBS → Settings → Stream → Service: Custom.

Stream key anything (e.g. main)
When to pick this

Older OBS (before 30.2), multi-RTMP plugins, or non-OBS encoders. The real platform stream key goes in Step 2 — not in OBS.
